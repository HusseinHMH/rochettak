# Portfolio Case Study — Rochtetak

## Project
Rochtetak — AI-assisted, multi-sided digital healthcare ecosystem concept for the MENA region.

## Product problem and opportunity
Healthcare interactions are fragmented across clinic scheduling, patient records, prescriptions, pharmacy fulfillment, diagnostic services, hospital operations and insurance administration. Rochtetak's product vision is to connect these participants through coordinated digital workflows.

## Product direction
- B2B SaaS for doctors, clinics and hospitals
- B2C patient discovery, booking and health-record experience
- Partner workspaces for pharmacies, labs, insurers and advertisers
- Hybrid monetization through SaaS subscriptions, booking commissions, partner commissions, patient premium tiers, sponsored content and a future medical marketplace / payment platform

## Prototype contribution
The interactive demo maps the product vision into multi-role experiences and representative workflows: doctor operations, patient booking, pharmacy handoff, diagnostic scheduling, hospital operations, insurance claims, commerce, payments, content moderation and platform administration. It includes bilingual UI and scripted AI workflow assistance.

## Current evidence and limitations
This is a self-initiated concept and front-end prototype. It does not have verified customers, production users, revenue, live integrations, clinical validation or a production AI service. Demo metrics and records are fictional.

## Validation plan
1. Conduct 15 structured interviews across doctors, clinic managers, patients, pharmacists, lab operators and hospital administrators.
2. Test 10 representative user journeys with target users.
3. Measure task completion, time-on-task, comprehension, workflow friction and perceived value.
4. Prioritize an MVP around the highest-frequency validated pain points.
5. Seek 5 clinic leads and 5 demo requests as targets, not achieved outcomes.
6. Use findings to refine ICP, pricing, onboarding and go-to-market.
